Privacy policy

Effective date: October 2026

At BULLET IT Solutions, your privacy and the security of your data are our highest priorities. This Privacy Policy outlines how we collect, use, process, and protect your information when you use our enterprise IT services and website.

1. Information We Collect

We collect information that you voluntarily provide to us when expressing an interest in obtaining information about us or our products and services. This may include:

  • Personal Data: Name, email address, phone number, and company details provided through our contact forms.
  • Technical Data: IP addresses, browser types, and usage data automatically collected when you navigate our website to improve our infrastructure.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect or receive to:

  • Provide, operate, and maintain our enterprise IT solutions.
  • Respond to your inquiries and offer dedicated support.
  • Analyze website usage to optimize our digital presence.
  • Send administrative information, updates, and security alerts.

3. Data Security (The Bullet Standard)

As a technology company focused on high-level architecture and security, we implement robust, industry-standard security measures to protect your personal information. We utilize zero-trust architectures, end-to-end encryption, and secure server environments to prevent unauthorized access, data loss, or breaches.

4. Sharing Your Information

We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal identification information to others. We may share generic aggregated demographic information not linked to any personal identification information with our trusted business partners and infrastructure providers solely for the purposes outlined above.

5. Your Rights

Depending on your region, you may have rights to request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data. If you wish to exercise any of these rights, please contact us.

6. Contact Us

If you have questions or comments about this policy, you may email us at bullet.itsolutions@gmail.com or contact us via our main website form.
